$\ce{ H2SO4 + 2NaOH \rightarrow 2H2O + 2Na+ + SO4^2- }$

$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta H(\ce{H+ + OH- \rightarrow H2O}) &= -57,3 \frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{mol}} \\
\rightarrow \Delta H_N &= 2 \cdot \left( -57,3 \frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{mol}} \right) \\
&= -\Delta T \cdot c_p(\ce{H2O}) \cdot V \cdot \varrho(\ce{H2O}) \\
\rightarrow \Delta T &= \frac{114,6 \frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{mol}}}{4,2 \frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}\cdot\text{K}} \cdot 40 \cdot 10^{-4} \text{ m}^3 \cdot 1000 \frac{\text{kg}}{\text m^3}} \\
&= 6,8 \frac{\text K}{\text{mol}}
\end{align*}
