### Nr. 16.
$$
\begin{align*}
l_p &\colonequals 45 \text{ mm} \\
&= 0,045 \text{ m} \\
d &\colonequals 8 \text{ mm} \\
l_s &\colonequals 40 \text{ mm} \\
d_k &\colonequals 0,1 \text{ mm} \\
I &\colonequals 50 \text{ mA}
\end{align*}
$$

#### a.
$$
\begin{align*}
B &= \mu_0\mu_r \frac{NI}{l} \\
N &= \frac{l_s}{d_k} \\
&= 400 \\
\rightarrow B &\approx 62,8 ~\mu\text{T}
\end{align*}
$$

#### b.
$$
\begin{align*}
R &= \varrho \cdot \frac{l}{A} \\
&= \varrho \cdot \frac{4N\sqrt{\pi^2\left(d+\frac{d_k}2\right)^2 + d_k^2}}{\pi d_k^2} \\
&= \frac UI \\
\rightarrow U &= I\varrho \cdot \frac{4N\sqrt{\pi^2\left(d+\frac{d_k}2\right)^2 + d_k^2}}{\pi d_k^2} \\
&\approx 1.095.000 \text{ V}
\end{align*}
$$
