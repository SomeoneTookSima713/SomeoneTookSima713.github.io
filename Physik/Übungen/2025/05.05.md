## LB. S. 240
###### Anderes Lehrbuch
### Nr. 1.
$$
\begin{align*}
m &\colonequals 6,64 \cdot 10^{-27} \text{ kg} \\
U &\colonequals 200 \text{ V} \\
B &\colonequals 120 \text{ mT} \\
q &\colonequals 2e
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac vr &= \frac{qB}m \\
E &= \Delta \varphi \cdot q \\
E &= Uq \\
&= \frac12mv^2 \\
\rightarrow v &= \sqrt{ \frac{2Uq}{m} } \\
\rightarrow r &= \frac{ \sqrt{ \frac{2Uq}{m} }m }{qB} \\
&= \frac{\sqrt{2Uqm}}{qB} \\
&\approx 0,024 \text{ m} \\
&\approx 23,99 \text{ mm}
\end{align*}
$$
