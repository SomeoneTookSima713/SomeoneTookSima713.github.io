Blauer Laser:
$$
\begin{align*}
e &= 3,17 \text{ m} \\
s_k &= \frac{0,212}{2} \text{ m} \\
k &= 1 \\
b &= \frac{1}{80} \text{ mm} \\
&= \frac{1}{80.000} \text{ m}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\alpha &= \arctan\frac{s_k}{e} \\
&= \arcsin \frac{k\cdot\lambda}{b} \\
\rightarrow \frac{k\lambda}{b} &= \sin\arctan \frac{s_k}{e} \\
\rightarrow \lambda &= \frac{b\sin\arctan \frac{s_k}{e}}{k} \\
&\approx 417,75 \text{ nm}
\end{align*}
$$
